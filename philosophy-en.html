Our Philosophy

Fasting — from solitary endurance to a shared culture.

The wisdom of “time without eating,” handed down around the world, brought back into everyday life through yuimaru — Okinawa's spirit of helping one another. Here is what Yuimaru Fasting believes in, and the rules we live by.

OUR WORDS

Where It Starts

  1. One day I tripped on a flat road, and realized my legs no longer lifted the way they used to.

  2. Fasting is often seen as nothing more than painful self-denial.

  3. Yet around the world, it has long been handed down as everyday wisdom.

  4. Okinawa has its own: hara hachi bu (eat until 80% full), nuchi gusui (food as medicine), and yuimaru — helping one another.

  5. No one has to do it alone — not with the right knowledge and friends who walk beside you.

  6. From the island, we will make fasting part of everyday culture — one step at a time.

PHILOSOPHY

What We Believe

Connecting the time we don't eat — through yuimaru.

Fasting is not a passing diet trend. It has been carried through people's lives as prayer and spiritual practice, as medicine, and — on Okinawa, an island known for long life — as the habit of eating only until 80% full.

Today, though, extreme methods and poorly grounded information are everywhere, and fasting too easily becomes something you endure alone. With a pharmacist's knowledge and Okinawa's spirit of yuimaru — mutual help — we want to make fasting once again a piece of everyday wisdom that people can enjoy with peace of mind.

VISION / The future we aim for

Make fasting part of everyday culture.

An island — and a society — where “Let's leave 12 hours tonight” is something families and friends say to each other naturally.

MISSION / The role we play

From fasting alone, to fasting together.

We share accurate knowledge in plain words, create a place where people can show each other their records, and spread a system that helps everyone see it through.

CORE VALUE / What we hold dear

Right, safe, and steady.

Lasting matters more than speed. Feeling lighter in body and mind matters more than numbers. Through diet alone, we aim for about 2–3 kg a month.

8 RULES

Our Eight Rules

What we hold dear at Yuimaru Fasting

  1. 1

    Know it right

    Not vague hearsay, but knowledge grounded in research and evidence — shared in words anyone can understand.

  2. 2

    Safety first

    People with medical conditions or on medication, those who are pregnant or breastfeeding, and growing children should not push themselves. When in doubt, we encourage you to ask a doctor or pharmacist.

  3. 3

    Never alone

    When it gets hard, we reach out to each other. In the spirit of yuimaru, we see the period we chose through — together.

  4. 4

    Slow and steady

    No extreme methods. Start with 12 hours tonight. Stacking up small wins is the surest path.

  5. 5

    Show the record

    Our weight, and the days that didn't go well — we record them honestly and share them. The strength to continue comes from making progress visible.

  6. 6

    Honor island wisdom

    Hara hachi bu and nuchi gusui — “medicine for life.” We bring the long-lived island's way of eating and its herbal knowledge into life today.

  7. 7

    Enjoy the journey

    Smiles, not grim endurance. Fitting into the clothes you love, feeling lighter — we share those joys together.

  8. 8

    Pass it on

    Your experience is a signpost for the next person who tries. The support you received, you pass on to the next friend. That is how a culture is made.

MESSAGE

A Message from the Founder

Beyond losing weight: confidence and connection.

Working as a pharmacist in hospitals, a university hospital and community pharmacies, I felt again and again that even with the right knowledge, it is hard to keep going alone. I am one of those people too — I hit that same wall while facing my own body after giving birth.

In Okinawa we say “Ichariba chōdē” — once we meet, we are family. Whether you want to fit into the clothes you love or ease pain in your body, everyone's reason is different. But I believe that with friends heading toward the same goal, people can change.

Not as endurance, but as wisdom handed down through generations — from this island, we will spread a culture of right and safe fasting.

SHOKO, Founder of Yuimaru Fasting / Pharmacist
